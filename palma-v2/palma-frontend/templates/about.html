Behind the Vision of
IIUM PalmaCount

We are bridging the gap between traditional agriculture and deep learning technology to sustain Malaysia's palm oil industry.

The Vision

Our technology serves as a digital alternative to traditional on-ground surveys.

By utilizing YOLOv8 deep learning, we help major organizations monitor tree health, age classification, and area estimation with significantly higher accuracy and lower operational costs.

Project Objectives

  • 1 Develop robust deep learning algorithms for automated tree counting.
  • 2 Integrate high-resolution image analysis into a web-based platform.
  • 3 Provide real-time data visualization for plantation sustainability.
